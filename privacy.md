# Claire Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 12, 2026**

Claire is a dream interpretation app. Your dreams are personal, and we treat them that way. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do.

## What we collect

**Your dreams.** The dreams you record (voice or text) and the readings generated from them. This is the core of the app.

**Your profile.** The name you choose, how often you dream, and what is on your mind. Used only to personalize your readings.

**Usage analytics.** Which screens you visit and which features you use, tied to an anonymous identifier. This helps us understand what works and fix what does not.

**Purchase information.** If you subscribe, our payment partner processes the transaction. We never see or store your payment details. Apple handles all billing.

## How your dreams are processed

To generate a reading, your dream text is sent to an AI language model (OpenAI) through our secure server. Your dream is processed to create your reading and is not used to train AI models. Your name and chosen focus area are included so the reading speaks to you personally.

## Where your data lives

Your dreams and readings are stored on your device and backed up to our secure database (Supabase, hosted in the United States) under an anonymous account created automatically when you first open the app. Only you can access your data. If you sign in with Apple, your data becomes recoverable across devices.

## Who we share data with

We share data only with the services that make the app work:

- **OpenAI** processes dream text to generate readings
- **Supabase** stores your data securely
- **RevenueCat** manages subscriptions
- **Amplitude** provides anonymous usage analytics

We never sell your data. We never share your dreams with advertisers. No third party receives your dream content except OpenAI, solely to generate your reading.

## Your choices

- **Delete your dreams:** Settings > Clear all dreams removes them from your device and our servers.
- **Use without an account:** the app works fully without signing in. Anonymous accounts are created automatically and contain no personal identifiers unless you add them.
- **Notifications:** entirely optional and configurable in Settings.

## Data retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your dreams removes them permanently from our servers. Anonymous accounts with no activity may be removed after extended inactivity.

## Children

Claire is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
