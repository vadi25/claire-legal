# Claire Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 23, 2026**

Claire is a dream interpretation app. Your dreams are personal, and we treat them that way. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do.

## What we collect

**Your dreams.** The dream text you type or create through voice transcription, plus the readings generated from it. This is the core of the app.

**Your profile.** The name you choose, how often you dream, and what is on your mind. This is used to personalize your readings.

**Account information.** Claire uses Sign in with Apple. Apple may provide your email address, including a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email. Your account is identified in our systems by a pseudonymous user ID.

**Usage analytics.** Which screens you visit and which features you use, associated with a pseudonymous account identifier. This helps us understand what works and fix what does not.

**Purchase information.** If you subscribe, Apple processes the transaction and RevenueCat helps us verify your subscription status. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## Voice transcription

If you choose voice input, microphone audio is sent to Apple's Speech service to produce a transcript. Claire does not store the audio recording. The resulting transcript is handled like dream text as described below. You can always type a dream instead of granting microphone or speech-recognition access.

## How your dreams are processed

To generate a reading, your dream text is sent to OpenAI through our secure server. Your dream is processed only to create your reading and is not used to train AI models. Your chosen name and focus area are included so the reading can be personalized.

## Where your data lives

Your dreams and readings are stored on your device and backed up to Supabase, hosted in the United States. They are associated with your Claire account so they can be restored across devices after Sign in with Apple.

## Who we share data with

We share data only with services needed to operate Claire:

- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple, speech transcription, and subscription billing
- **OpenAI** processes dream text to generate readings
- **Supabase** provides account authentication, secure storage, and server functions
- **RevenueCat** verifies and manages subscription access
- **Amplitude** provides product usage analytics

We never sell your data. We never share your dreams with advertisers.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account:** Settings > Delete account permanently removes your Claire account and its associated profile, dreams, readings, and symbol map from our systems and clears that data from the device. Apple subscription billing is managed separately, and the app links directly to Apple's subscription management screen before deletion.
- **Notifications:** Notifications are optional and configurable in Settings and iOS Settings.
- **Voice input:** Microphone and speech-recognition access are optional. You may type dreams instead.

## Data retention

We keep your account data while your Claire account exists. When you delete your account in the app, the associated account and personal content are permanently removed from our systems. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records as required for billing, fraud prevention, tax, or legal compliance.

## Children

Claire is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date and, when appropriate, notify you in the app.
